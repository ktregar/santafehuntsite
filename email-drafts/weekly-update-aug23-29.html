Thomas’ Hunting Diary
Help Us Panel the Costo Fixture

The pack cooling off at Costo — one of our existing coops sits on the fence line behind.

We have all heard the adage that people either “ride to hunt” or “hunt to ride” — but regardless of your motivation, sufficient and safe paneling is a necessity in any fixture.

We need to upgrade the paneling in the Costo fixture as we begin to hunt over the next few weeks, and we need your help. The staff and volunteers will provide the labor to construct and place the coops — but lumber is desperately needed.

What a Coop Takes
›Seven 1x6″ boards on either side, over a 2x4 substructure. Height is determined by the angle and width of the base.
›Seasoned native lumber — or lumber treated to withstand years of weather with minimal maintenance.
›What we’re looking for: 10–12′ lengths of 1x6.

There are three ways you can help:

›Have lumber that fits? Let us know what you’ve got.
›Know where we can get it? Point us in the right direction.
›Willing to support the purchase? We’d be grateful for the help.

Growing our membership is critical to our future, and providing good sport — allowing staff to stay with hounds in country that is properly paneled — will go a long way toward making that happen.

Not to mention the fun for those who like to jump!
