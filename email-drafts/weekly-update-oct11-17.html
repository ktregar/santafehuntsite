Saturday, October 17 · Garner Ranch
The Clinic Is Saturday

Our Mock Hunt & Foxhunting Clinic is the best way we know to try riding to hounds: three fields from hilltopping to jumping, a walk through the traditions and turnout, and lunch together afterward. Any discipline, any experience level, and juniors welcome.

RSVP by Monday, October 12. Please arrive by 8:30 a.m.; the program starts at 9:00. The ranch address goes out to registered riders.

If you have a friend who’s been curious, this is the day to bring them.

The Hunter Pace Is Now October 25

With the storm headed our way, we moved the Halloween Hunter Pace to Sunday, October 25, still at Costo. If you’d already entered, you should have an email from us with your options.

The silver lining: two more weeks to put a team together. Entries are open through Wednesday, October 21, and costumes are still encouraged. Starts are open-gate: arrive by 8:30 a.m. to check in, and the first team goes out at 9:00.
