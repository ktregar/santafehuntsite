The Clinic Is Saturday

Our Mock Hunt & Foxhunting Clinic is the best way we know to try riding to hounds: three fields from hilltopping to jumping, a walk through the traditions and turnout, and lunch together afterward. Any discipline, any experience level, and juniors welcome.

RSVP by Monday, October 12. Please arrive by 8:30 a.m.; the program starts at 9:00. The ranch address goes out to registered riders.

If you have a friend who’s been curious, this is the day to bring them.

From the Kennel
Three Couple of New Hounds

A note from our Honorary Huntsman:

“Thanks to the generosity of friends in the Midwest and Northeast, and to our road warrior Talena, we now have three couple of new, unentered hounds to round out our pack and diversify our bloodlines. I’m looking forward to integrating them into the kennel and getting them out on exercise and hunting as soon as the weather will allow.”

— Dr. Steven Thomas, ex-MFH, Honorary Huntsman

That’s six hounds (hounds are counted in couples), five females and one male. “Unentered” means they haven’t hunted yet; this will be their first season.

And after the storm: the kennel came through the rain just fine, and the whole pack, new arrivals included, is warm and dry inside. Photos of the newcomers to come.
