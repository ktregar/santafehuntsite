Registration Is Open
Two dates worth clearing your calendar for

Our two big autumn events are open for entries. Both are open to the public, so bring friends — and note the deadlines, because a couple of them come round sooner than you’d think.

A team from a past pace. Costumes are encouraged — horses too, if the mood takes you.

Sunday, October 11 · Costo Ranch, Anza
Halloween Hunter Pace

Teams of two or three ride out through open country over optional fences, competing to finish closest to an ideal time. Four divisions — Hunt, Pleasure, Junior and Western — so there’s a pace to suit every horse and rider. Vendors, lunch and ribbons to follow.

Adult member · junior member$65 · $50
Adult non-member · junior non-member$95 · $80
Lunch only, no ride$25

Every rider entry includes a Hunter Pace t-shirt. Register by Friday, September 25 to be guaranteed your size — after that we’ll fit you from spare stock. Entries close Sunday, October 4.

Saturday, October 17 · Garner Ranch
Mock Hunt & Foxhunting Clinic

The best day of the year to bring a friend who has never hunted. Riders of any discipline are welcome, and there are three fields — jumping up to three feet, jumping optional, or hilltoppers with no jumping at all, which is really just a nice trail ride in good company. You’ll learn the tradition, the etiquette and the lingo, and there’s a luncheon afterwards.

Juniors very welcome — bring the kids.

Adult member · junior member$40 · $30
Lunch only, no ride$30

Every rider entry includes a Santa Fe Hunt cap. Lunch and the ranch fee are included too. RSVP by Monday, October 12 — the address for the day goes out to registered riders.
