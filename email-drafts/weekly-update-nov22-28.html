Last Saturday · Costo Ranch
Five Coyotes in One Morning

Saturday at Costo was quite a day. After a frosty night with lows in the high 20s, hounds moved off at 9:00 and soon opened, running fast into the deep covert along Terwilliger Road. By the time we hacked home nearly three hours later, the field had viewed five coyotes.

One headed toward the Pump House, where Talena turned him back into the country for hounds. Another literally ran circles around the pack in the covert south of the Demolition Yard. The best came last: the first field viewed a coyote heading north, and hounds were taken to the line and found quickly. Constance led the pack, every hound on and opening hard, and staff galloped ahead to hold them up before the heavy covert. Three couple of puppies were out, and this very young pack is coming along beautifully.

A small first field was led by new member Taylor, with Christina, our guest from Sierra Nevada Hounds, riding up. Laurie, Kathleen and Amy rode in second field. Thank you to Talena, Courtney, Jim and Dani for whipping in.
