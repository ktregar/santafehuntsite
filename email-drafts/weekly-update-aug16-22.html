Hunting Community
Supporting Sierra Nevada Hounds

Our friends at Sierra Nevada Hounds, near Reno and Lake Tahoe, were recently evacuated as wildfire moved through their area. As they work toward returning home and rebuilding, the Sierra Nevada Hounds Foundation — a registered 501(c)(3) — is accepting donations to support their recovery.

The foxhunting community is a small one, and we’re keeping them and their hounds in our thoughts. If you’re able to help, every bit makes a difference.
