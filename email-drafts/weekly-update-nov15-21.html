Opening Hunt · Garner Ranch
The 2026–27 Season Is Open

Thank you to everyone who turned out for Opening Hunt on Saturday. It was a cool, overcast morning at the beautiful and historic Garner Ranch, and a nice crowd of riders formed three fields after a welcome from Master Terry Paine.

Hounds moved off at 10:00 and were put into the creek bed, hunting south through the willows. Our young pack, mostly new entry, hunted hard all the way to the end of the territory and worked beautifully together, led by Constance, on a line that had gone a bit old with the game already up in the hills. Six couple of bitches went out, three couple of them puppies.

After a final water stop at the pond, everyone headed to Kim and Marian’s home for lunch, and the hounds went home to a well-deserved dinner of their own. Thank you to Kim and Marian for hosting, and to Terry, Talena, Courtney and Jim for whipping in.

Photos by Alisa Brandt.

Naming Auction
Meet Junie!

Junie, short for Juniper.

Our newest hound has a name! Congratulations to Amelie Barratt, whose winning bid of $225 earned her the honor of naming her. Amelie chose Junie, short for Juniper, and Steve has given it his approval.

Thank you to everyone who bid in our first hound-naming auction. Bids went back and forth right up to the last day, and every dollar goes to the care and keep of the pack.
