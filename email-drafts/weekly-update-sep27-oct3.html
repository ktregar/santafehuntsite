Volunteers Wanted · October 17
Help Us Pass It On

Every fall the Mock Hunt & Foxhunting Clinic brings a few new faces to Garner Ranch, and the best teachers they’ll find are the members who already know the country and the traditions. If that’s you, we’d be grateful for your help, on the ground or in the saddle.

On the ground
Check-in, and turnout on display. We need people to check riders in, and people to show what proper turnout looks like: with and without colors, for men and for women, formal and informal season, and staff. Seeing it on a real person teaches more than any diagram.
On horseback
Lead a field, or ride along in one. Field leaders, plus experienced riders in each field to explain what’s happening as it happens, answer questions, and step in if a newcomer needs a hand.

To volunteer, reply to this email or text the hunt and tell us which you’d like to do.
