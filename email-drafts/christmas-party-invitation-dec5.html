A Hunting Tradition
The Huntsman’s Purse

The Huntsman’s Purse is a long-standing foxhunting tradition. Once a year, usually at Christmas, members of a hunt give a cash gift to the kennelsman, huntsman and first whip to thank them for all the extras these tireless people do.

In our hunt, members put whatever cash they would like to give in an envelope and hand it to Master Paine at the party. Master Paine combines the gifts and ceremoniously presents the purse to Talena, in recognition of her unending devotion to our hounds and all she does for the Santa Fe West Hills Hounds.

Cash only, please. No checks.
