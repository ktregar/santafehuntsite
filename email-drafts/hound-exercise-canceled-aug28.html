Paw Safety
The Ground Is Hotter Than the Air

On a sunny day, asphalt runs far hotter than the temperature on your phone. This is why we call off exercise on days that may not sound that bad:

Air TemperatureAsphalt Reaches
77°F→125°F
 
87°F→143°F
 
95°F→149°F

At 125°F, skin damage can occur in about 60 seconds.

The Seven-Second Test

Press the back of your hand flat against the pavement and hold it there for seven seconds. If you have to pull away, it’s too hot for your dog’s feet — and for our hounds.
