Five Questions with Gideon Kotkowski

Mongol Derby finisher, host of the Horse People Podcast, and a man on a mission to try every horse discipline in the world.

Photo: The Equestrianists

What drew you to foxhunting?

I met Jim through the Mongolia crowd, and he invited me out. It sounded like a good time right away, since foxhunting means going fast across open country and that’s always where I want to be. A bunch of the people I rode the Derby with are foxhunters too, so between the company and the pace I was sold. I’d never done it before, and it just sounded fun.

What’s the most challenging ride you’ve done?

The Mongol Derby, without a doubt. A thousand kilometers across the open Mongolian steppe on semi-wild horses, twelve-plus hours a day in the saddle on these little horses that my frame does not fit. Nothing else I’ve done comes anywhere close. A few spicy polo ponies have tested me over the years, and that’s the nearest thing I can point to, but it’s still a distant second.

Out hunting with Jim, who first invited him along.

On the polo field — a distant second to Mongolia.

If you could go anywhere for your next riding adventure, where would you go?

Right now I’m craving Patagonia. I’d head down there and ride with all the buddies, Erik Cooper, Jim, whoever else wants to come from up here. After that I’ve got my eye on New Zealand. I heard Steve talk about foxhunting with the Kiwis and jumping more than forty times in a single day, and that sounds like an absolute blast.

Who’s been a favorite or most surprising podcast guest so far?

A few really stand out. Filipe Masetti Leite rode from Alaska all the way down to Ushuaia, Argentina over the course of two years, on mustangs and horses he trained up for the trip. That’s around episode 14 and one of my favorites. I also loved sitting down with Renate Larson, a PhD ethnologist focused on horses, who brings this fascinating angle on the culture and history that humans and horses share. That one lands somewhere in the twenties. Most recently I talked with Steve Thomas, the honorary huntsman here at Santa Fe Hounds. If you’re reading this and want to listen, the link is below.

You’re on a mission to try every horse discipline in the world. What’s still on the list?

The list is still long, which is the whole point. I’ll be filming at the Santa Fe opening hunt, and from there we start figuring out which disciplines we can actually get out to and turn into good content. Jousting is high up, and mounted archery would be a blast. I want proper episodes on showjumping, eventing, and polo, plus all the Western stuff, rodeo, roping, the works. There’s just so much left to go try.

Where it all started: Gideon with La Morena, his first horse.
