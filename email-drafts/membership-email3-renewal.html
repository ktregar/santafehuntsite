Hounds go into Costo
on the 8th

Hello —

On Tuesday, September 8, staff take hounds into the Costo fixture and the season starts in earnest. We’d love it if you were part of it again.

A fair bit has changed since you last rode with us, and we think it’s changed for the better:

›Dr. Steven Thomas has come on as our Honorary Huntsman, and has been working the pack hard all summer.
›Payden Steele joined us as Professional Kennelman.
›The kennel is being rebuilt — a new wash and medical station, the yards repaired, and more room for the hounds to stretch out.
›We’re paneling the Costo fixture with new coops, so staff can stay with hounds and there’s proper country to ride.

Hounds have been out on summer exercise every week. The work has been going in. What’s missing is the field.

Opening Hunt is November 14 at Garner Ranch. There’s time to get you and your horse legged up before then — but it starts now.

And if this isn’t your season — a horse laid up, a year that got away from you, life being life — we’d still like to hear from you. Tell us what’s going on and we’ll keep you posted for next year.

You’re also welcome at hound exercise any Tuesday or Friday morning, member or not. No horse required, and the hounds are always glad of the company.

We hope to see you out this season.

Warm regards,

Marian Cerilly
President, Santa Fe Hunt
