Opening Hunt Is Saturday

Our formal season begins this Saturday with the stirrup cup at 9:15, the Blessing of the Hounds at 9:45, and hounds moving off at 10:00. A catered lunch follows hunting. Here’s what to take care of this week.

1. RegisterPlease register online as soon as you can, riders and lunch guests alike, so we have an accurate count for lunch.
2. PayMembers $65, juniors $50, adult non-members $245 (includes your day membership), lunch only $50. The online form takes PayPal with a small handling fee. To skip the fee, Zelle us at santafehounds@gmail.com with your name and “Opening Hunt” in the memo.
Reading this on a computer? Scan with your phone’s camera or banking app to pay Santa Fe Hunt by Zelle.
3. WaiverNon-members, please sign the digital waiver before Saturday. It takes two minutes and saves time at the trailers.
4. BraidHorses should be braided for Opening. Plan your morning so you’re mounted and ready well before the stirrup cup.

Dressing for Opening

Opening is the first formal hunt of the season, so autumn tweeds go back in the closet. Here’s formal attire at a glance.

CoatBlack hunting coat, frock coat or black shadbelly. Members with colors wear their scarlet or navy coat with the Sutter’s Gold collar.
StockWhite stock tie, neatly tied, with a plain pin worn horizontally and the ends pinned down.
Shirt and vestWhite shirt (canary with white collar and cuffs is fine). Canary or tattersall vest.
BreechesBeige or buff. Members with colors wear white.
BootsPlain black hunting boots without laces. Staff and gentlemen with colors wear black boots with brown tops; ladies with colors may wear black dress boots with patent leather tops.
HelmetBlack ASTM-certified helmet, harness fastened, ribbon up. Hair neatly contained in a hairnet.
GlovesBuff, brown or black leather, or white string gloves.
