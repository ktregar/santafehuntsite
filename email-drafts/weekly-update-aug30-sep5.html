This Monday
Work Day at the Kennel

We’re getting the kennel squared away this Monday, August 31, and we’d be glad of any hands that can make it. Apologies for the short notice — come for an hour or come for the day, whatever you can spare.

On the List
›Filling in holes in the kennel yard
›Installing the new wash and medical station
›Patching concrete
›Clearing the fence and patching holes in the back kennel yard, so the hounds can have more room to stretch out
›General cleaning and organizing

Painting is on the list for later — we need to wait until it cools off enough to close the windows and keep the dust out while it dries.

Looking For Donations

Before you take it to the dump — we could use any of the following:

›A desk chair for the kennel office
›Used plastic playground equipment
›A kiddie pool

The last two are for the kennel yard — hounds get bored, and something to climb on and splash in goes a long way.
