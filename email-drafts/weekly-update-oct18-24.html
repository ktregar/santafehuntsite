Tuesday, October 20 · Costo · 8:00 a.m.
Autumn Hunting Begins Tuesday

The informal season, known as autumn hunting or “cubbing,” starts this week. From now until Opening Hunt on November 14, hounds go out on Tuesdays and Saturdays, and you’re invited to come out with them. Friday hound exercise carries on through the season too.

These are relaxed, early-morning days. The focus is on bringing the young hounds along (including our six new arrivals), so expect a quieter pace than a formal hunt day. Informal ratcatcher attire is the dress: tweed or a plain coat, a light shirt and a stock tie or necktie.

Non-members are welcome too: the capping fee for an autumn hunting day is $75. Meets are announced week by week, and most are at Costo. Let Steve know if you plan to come out.

Sunday, October 25 · Costo Ranch
The Pace Is a Week Away

Our rescheduled Halloween Hunter Pace is next Sunday. Entries close Wednesday, October 21, so there’s still time to put a team of two or three together. Every rider gets a t-shirt, and costumes are encouraged.

Starts are open-gate: arrive by 8:30 a.m. to check in, the first team goes out at 9:00, and lunch and ribbons follow. We can always use another volunteer on the day. Reply to help.
