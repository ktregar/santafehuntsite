The Rules of the Auction

Syllables, Ideally
  • Highest bid wins. Bidding opens at $150 and goes up in $25 steps. We post the current high bid on this page as bids come in.
  • Her name must start with J. Hounds in a litter share a first letter, and hers is J. Two-syllable names carry best across the hunt field, like Juno, Jewel or Jessie.
  • Your name idea stays private. We show only the bid amount, never the bidder or the name, until the winner is announced. We'll reveal her name at the Blessing of the Hounds on Opening Day, Saturday, November 14.
  • The Master and Huntsman have final approval. If they can't approve the winning name, we'll work with the winner on another J name they love.
  • The winner pays after bidding closes. We'll contact you by email with payment instructions.
  • You can bid as a group. Team up with friends, pool your bid and agree on a name together. One person places the bid and is our contact for payment.
  • If there's a tie, the earlier bid wins. If the winner hasn't paid within one week, the honor passes to the next-highest bidder.

Paying: Zelle to santafehounds@gmail.com (no fee) or PayPal (a small handling fee applies). All proceeds go to the care and keep of the pack.
