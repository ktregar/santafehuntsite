    How It Works

    The Details

    • First come, first served. Tell us your first and second choice. We confirm each coop by email in the order requests arrive, then mark it as claimed here.
    • Name it for anyone. Yourself, your family, your farm, a favorite horse or someone you'd like to remember. The Master has final approval, and we'll work with you if a name can't be used.
    • How long it lasts. Your name stays on the coop as long as the coop stands and the Hunt rides at Costo.
    • Collars go where they're needed. The Huntsman chooses which hound wears each collar, and a collar may move between hounds as the pack changes.
    • Pay after we confirm. We'll email you payment instructions. If we don't receive payment within one week, the coop goes back on the list.

    Paying: Zelle to santafehounds@gmail.com (no fee) or PayPal (a small handling fee applies). Proceeds support the paneling at Costo and the care of the pack.
