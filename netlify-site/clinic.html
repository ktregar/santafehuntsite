Have you seen foxhunting on Downton Abbey and thought it looked like fun, but haven't had a chance to try it? Do you enjoy riding with friends out in beautiful open spaces? Are you interested in learning more about riding to hounds?

If so, come join the Santa Fe Hunt for our mock hunt and foxhunting clinic. This is a very unique experience for anyone who has never done this before, and a good refresher for those who have. Go at your own pace and enjoy the open space with your horse. Learn about the tradition, etiquette, lingo, and history of the fox hunt.

Most of all, expect to have fun.

Come Prepared

What to Wear & Bring

Rider Attire
  • Long sleeve shirt with a collar — any color except red
  • Helmets required
  • Boots or shoes with a heel, and half chaps
Horse & Kit
  • English or western tack — please, no bling
  • Bring hay, buckets, and water for your horse
  • Pack a chair for our informative luncheon afterwards
