Join us for our Halloween Hunter Pace. In this East-Coast-style event, teams of two or three riders are invited to ride through open countryside and over optional fences, competing to finish the course closest to the ideal time. There will be vendors and a delicious lunch following the ride, with ribbons awarded at the luncheon.

Costumes are encouraged — it is a Halloween pace, after all! Dress up your horse too if the mood takes you. ASTM-certified helmets are required for all riders, over or under whatever else you have on.

Choose Your Ride

The Four Divisions

There are four divisions, offering an option for every skill level and riding style. Each division is set to its own ideal time, and the guidelines below describe the pace expected over different footing.

Fastest Time

Hunt Division

  • Time — This division has the fastest time
  • Walk — Deep sand, very rocky footing, when passing other teams
  • Trot — Trappy or winding tracks, rocky footing
  • Canter — Winding tracks on good footing
  • Gallop — Open straightaways
Relaxed Time

Pleasure Division

  • Time — This division has a more relaxed time
  • Walk — Deep sand, poor footing, winding tracks, when passing other teams
  • Trot — Winding tracks on good footing
  • Canter — Open straightaways
Most Relaxed Time

Junior Division

  • Time — The most relaxed time, to allow riders extra time making choices about pace and jumps
  • Walk — Deep sand, poor footing, winding tracks, when passing other teams
  • Trot — Winding tracks on good footing
  • Canter — Open straightaways
Moderate Pace

Western Division

  • Time — This division has a moderate pace
  • Walk — Deep sand, poor footing, when passing other teams
  • Trot — Winding tracks on fair footing
  • Canter — Winding tracks on good footing, some open straightaways
  • Gallop — Open straightaways on good footing
